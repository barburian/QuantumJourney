Click a gate to chain it onto the selected block. Drag pin to pin to wire. Drag the background to pan, scroll to zoom. Drop a Probe on a wire to inspect it. Wires: thickness = P(1), colour = phase, glow = entangled. Shift-click or shift-drag to select several blocks, Ctrl+G to make them a gate, double-click a gate to edit it. ⊞ on a gate shows its matrix. Orange wires carry classical values: drop one on a gate to make it conditional.
